# MediaBrowseScaffold

The library wall shared by Movies, Series, Music and Readarr's Authors: glass app bar, search field, KPI peek, filter chips with a pinned sort control, and a lettered poster wall that becomes a result grid while searching.

Rendered from `lib/core/widgets/media_browse_scaffold.dart` (with `lib/core/widgets/search_bar_header.dart`; results are `MediaGrid`, the peek is `ServiceKpiPeek`) by `test/design_catalog/media_browse_scaffold_catalog_test.dart`.

- **Page**: an `AmbientScaffold` in the service's accent (the lamp), with the body in the `AppBreakpoints.contentWell` (1140) well. The header (search, peek, chips) sits in a `NestedScrollView`'s header slivers, so it scrolls away with the wall rather than staying fixed.
- **App bar**: `GlassAppBar` (the `GlassSurface` gradient, no blur), `kToolbarHeight` (56), title in `title-large` 600 `on-surface`, one action: `Icons.history_rounded` (tooltip *Activity*) pushes `activityRoute`. While searching a leading `Icons.arrow_back_rounded` (tooltip *Exit search*) clears the query. `showAppBar: false` drops it for hosts that bring their own chrome, and `topPadding` reserves that much space, plus the top safe area, above the header.
- **Search** (`SearchBarHeader`): padding `space-sm` top, `space-lg` sides and bottom. A Material `SearchBar`, min height 52, `radius-md`, `surface-container` fill with a 1px `outline-variant` edge, padding `space-sm`; leading `Icons.search_rounded` inset `space-sm`, in `on-surface-variant` (monochrome, on every screen). With text, a trailing `Icons.close_rounded` clear button in `on-surface-variant`. Input is debounced 400ms. In the desktop shell the field registers as the screen's ⌘F target.
- **KPI peek** (hidden while searching): whatever `kpiPeek` the screen passes, in practice a `ServiceKpiPeek` of the service's figures.
- **Filter strip** (hidden while searching): padding `space-sm` top, `space-lg` sides, `space-md` bottom. Chips scroll, `space-sm` apart: padding `space-sm` × `space-md`, `radius-pill`, `label-medium` 600. Selected: the ink fill (`primary`), label `on-primary` by `ServiceTheme.foregroundOn`. Unselected: the ink at 12% with the label measured against that wash on the page surface: the brand book's chip, the same whichever service owns the library. Chips: *All*, *Available*, *Missing*, *In queue*, *Cutoff unmet* (only when `cutoffUnmetIds` is non-null, and an empty set still draws it), *Unmonitored*. *Available* and *Missing* partition the library through `mediaBrowseBucketOf`; *In queue*, *Cutoff unmet* and *Unmonitored* are orthogonal to that partition. *Available*, *Missing*, *In queue* and *Unmonitored* put a question to each title's status, so they are drawn only when the screen passes a `statusExtractor`: a title with no status reads as a gap, which would answer *Missing* for the whole library and the others for none of it. A strip with one chip (*All*) draws no chips, as one order draws no sort control; with neither chips nor sort the strip is not drawn at all.
- **Sort control**: pinned right of the scrolling chips and built only when there are two or more orders: *Title A–Z* (always first, the default) plus the screen's `sorts` (`MediaBrowseSort.dateAdded`, `.year`, `.size`, or a custom one). A `PopupMenuButton` (tooltip *Change sort order*) whose child is at least 44 × 44, padding `space-sm`: `Icons.swap_vert_rounded` 18, `space-xs`, then the order's label in `AppTheme.chrome` 600, both `on-surface-variant`.
- **Wall**: list padding `space-sm` top, `space-lg` sides, plus the nav bar's clearance at the foot on a phone. Under a `lettered` order (only Title A–Z) each first letter of the sort title, or `#` for anything else, heads a section in `title-large` 600 with `space-md` below it; sections are `space-xl` apart. Any other order is one unheaded run. Columns come from `MediaGrid.columnsFor` over the row's width (never fewer than 3), 12 between columns, 16 between rows, and the tiles take the width that is left: about 111 on a 390 phone, about 148 in the 1140 well.
- **Poster tile**: a 2 : 3 `ContentCard` under its own `Hero`, with an 8px `StatusBadge(iconOnly: true)` dot at 6 / 6 from the lower-left corner; `space-xs`; the caption (`MediaPosterCaption` in `lib/core/widgets/media_poster_caption.dart`, shared with a media server's walls): the title in `label-small` 600 `on-surface` and the subtitle (the year) in `label-small` `on-surface-variant`, one line each, ellipsised.
- **`posterFallbackIcon`**: the glyph on a poster tile with no artwork, the film's clapper by default; Sonarr passes `Icons.tv_outlined`.
- **`tileShape: MediaBrowseTileShape.circle`** (Lidarr's wall): a square `MediaPosterCard.circular` with `Icons.person_outline_rounded` as its fallback, captions centred, and no dot: a circle has no corner to hold one, so the caption carries completeness in words.
- **Searching**: any non-empty query swaps the wall for a `MediaGrid` of `searchResultsProvider`: compact badges top-right, no captions. An empty answer reads *No results*, and only an answer does: the section searches ask their arr to report a failure (`reportFailure`), so a search that failed is an `AppErrorState` naming the service (the classified state and sentence, *Retry*), never the exception and never *No results*; loading is a centred spinner in the primary ink (the accent is the lamp, which `onTint` resolves to ink; the theme's own when no `accentColor` is given).
- **Library states**: the wall goes through `AsyncValueWidget`: a centred spinner while loading (the scaffold hands it as `loadingWidget`, in the same ink, as is the pull-to-refresh spinner), on a failure with nothing retained `ServiceOfflineState.failure` when the screen passes `service:` (the cause in plain words, the address, *Retry* and *Settings*, which Movies, Series, Music and Authors all do) and `AppErrorState` with *Retry* otherwise, `NotConfiguredPlaceholder` when the provider throws `<Service> not configured`, and a `FaultBand.unreachable` over the last wall it drew when a refresh fails. An empty library reads *Your <service> library is empty* (the screen's `emptyIcon` over it, and its `emptyMessage` under it when it has one: Authors says where an author comes from); a chip that matches nothing reads its own words with *Show all*. So `libraryProvider` must throw on a failed fetch: one that degrades to `[]` makes a dark server read as an empty library (the Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr walls did, until they moved to `get…OrThrow`).

The consumer provides `title` (*Movies*), `searchHint` (*Search movies*), `serviceName`, optionally `service` (the `ServiceKey`, for the failure leaf), `accentColor`, `emptyIcon` and `emptyMessage`, `activityRoute`, `navigationSection`, the hero tag prefixes for the wall and for search, the library, query and search-result providers, the settings selector for image auth, extractors for title, subtitle, sort title, images, id and status, `onItemTap`, the orders it can answer, the cutoff id set and the KPI peek.

Semantics: a tile is one button named by its full title, with *subtitle, status* as its value (*1922, Available*); the truncated caption and the dot are excluded. Chips are buttons carrying a selected state. The sort control's hint rides on its child (*Sorted by Title A–Z*), because a hint wrapped around a button is dropped. The search field is labelled *Search*.

Do: declare only the orders your model can answer, and pass `cutoffUnmetIds` as null until the service has answered. Don't: pin a tile width: the row resolves the count first and the tiles take what is left.

## States in the catalog

Each sheet is drawn in the dark and the light room; the wall also in a 1280 desktop window.

- The library under Title A–Z, Lidarr's circular wall, no sort choices and no cutoff source.
- A wall with no status to resolve (Readarr's Authors): the sort control alone on the strip and no status chips, the strip not drawn when there is no sort either, and an empty library with its own glyph and line.
- Searching with a result, with no match, an empty library, and results pending.
- Loading, a failed load with no service, the same failure with `service:`, not configured.
- Text at 200%: the library wall, a failed load with `service:` and a search.
