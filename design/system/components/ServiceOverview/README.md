# ServiceOverview

The furniture every service's Overview tab is built from: identity line, section frame, two-line row, empty note and skeleton, all derived from one `ServiceKey`.

Rendered from `lib/core/widgets/service_overview.dart`, as `lib/features/movies/presentation/radarr_overview_screen.dart` composes it, by `test/design_catalog/service_overview_catalog_test.dart`. Sixteen Overviews build on it; Radarr is the one shown. Titles, hosts and figures are sample data; section names, subtitles, links and empty copy are Radarr's real strings.

- **The page** (`ServiceOverviewPage`): the shell fourteen Overviews share (Stream's keeps its own, for its visit's connection and its one state for a dark server). The gate is the outer widget: a service not configured draws `NotConfiguredPlaceholder.forService` and nothing under it reaches a provider. The body is the scroll view, the identity line, `ServiceKpiPeek.room` (the service's own readout, or the source the page already watches) and the sections, under `sectionsOrOneLine`; a pull is `invalidateServiceReadout` (the probe and the figures' sources) and then what the page folds its sections from. It sits in `AmbientScaffold` under a `SafeArea`, and `test/features/services/presentation/service_tab_inset_test.dart` measures it once for all of them.
- **Page order** (Radarr): `ServiceOverviewIdentity` → `ServiceKpiPeek` (see that card; composed by the screen, not this file) → Coming up → Needs attention → Recently added → History. One scroll view (`ServiceOverviewScroll`): every section stays built, never a lazy list, since a section collected off screen came back from its skeleton and the page jumped under the thumb. It runs under the floating bar to the screen's foot, cleared by `getScrollViewBottomPadding`, never stopped by a `SafeArea` at the bar's edge (`test/core/widgets/bar_clearance_sweep_test.dart`). On the phone the app bar and the tab bar float above as glass bands and the page reserves `kToolbarHeight + ServiceTabBar.height` (56 + 44) at the top; in a desktop window `ServiceTabScaffold` draws the lit `ServiceTabBar.header` instead and lifts the page by the 56pt band it no longer draws. The page sits in `AmbientScaffold`, so a desktop window caps it at the content well.
- **Identity** (the room's status line): padding `space-sm` top, `space-lg` sides, `space-md` bottom. A 7pt dot in the connection tone (`statusToneColor`), 6, then one line in the meta role (13) in the secondary ink: the status word, then `host · version` (*Answering · nas.home.arpa:7878 · v5.27*). A figure the service did not give reads `—` (the version is `—` whenever the probe did not answer, never the `v3 API` fallback). The words are the classified ones: *Answering* (green), *Checking* while the probe is in flight, *Did not answer* (red) only when nothing answered, *Refused the key*, *Timed out* and the other faults (amber); a connection saved without its key is not asked, and reads *Not set up*, neutral, never *Did not answer*. A lens chip (padding `space-sm` × 2, `surface-container`, 1px `outline-variant`, `radius-sm`, `chrome` 11 / 600 in the accent walked for `surface`) appears only when the service has more than one connection. The identity names the lens's connection unless the page is given another: Jellyfin and Plex pass the connection their visit is reading (`instanceId`, see *A media server's visit* below), and then the host, the status word and the chip are that connection's.
- **Section** (`ServiceOverviewSection`): a slab of the room on the page column (`room.slab`, 1px `line-hair`, `radius-slab` 26, padding `space-lg` sides and `space-md` top and bottom, `space-md` between slabs) holding a `SectionHeader.flush` (`title-large` 600, subtitle `body-small` tabular in `on-surface-variant`), `space-sm`, and the body. A subtitle that counts nothing (`0`, `0 requests waiting`) is not drawn: the empty note says it in words; nor is one that repeats the room's figure above (Bazarr's *Wanted subtitles* under *2 wanted*). `ServiceOverviewSection.bleed` is no slab: its heading on the page inset and a poster rail running to the window edge; where a pointer reaches the page and the rail runs past it, the heading carries the rail's back and forward after its link ([RailArrows](../RailArrows/README.md); the rail takes their scroll from `RailArrows.controllerOf`). A section with a destination tab draws `Calendar` and an 18pt `chevron_right` in the label role 600 in `on-surface-variant` (the secondary ink, the brand book's section link), one line; the whole header is the tap target, at least 44 tall, and switches branch with `goBranch` (outside a shell it does nothing). A section whose rows belong to several tabs (Needs attention) has no link: its rows are doors one by one. A section about titles (Radarr's and Sonarr's Coming up, Seerr's Needs a decision, Bazarr's Wanted subtitles, a media server's Now playing: see [NowPlaying](../NowPlaying/README.md)) is given its first title's light (`light:`, the title's `RoomArt`) and glows with it inside its slab (`SlabLight`, at 0.5), as the Home's Coming up does; a rail has no slab and takes none.
- **Shared by the four \*arr Overviews** (`ArrNeedsAttentionSection`, `ArrHistoryTodaySection`, `ServiceAttentionRow`, `ServiceHistoryRow`, `ServicePosterRail`; their data is `lib/core/utils/arr_overview.dart`): what differs per service is the provider, the enum of faults, the subject of a record and, for the rail, a caption and what a tap opens, never a flag. Needs attention counts what the rows stand for through `hasValue`, so a source that failed over rows it still holds keeps its count, and a source that never answered reads `—`.
- **Poster rail** (Radarr's Recently added): tiles 96 wide, `space-md` apart, the rail inset `space-lg`. Each is a `MediaPosterCard` in a 96 × 138 box (`radius-md`, `level-2`, `BoxFit.cover`; no poster → `plate-surface` with `movie_outlined` 48 in `plate-dim-ink`), `space-xs`, title `label-small` 600 `on-surface` (one line, ellipsised), year `label-small` `on-surface-variant`. The tile is one button that speaks the title with the year as its value.
- **Row** (`ServiceOverviewRow`): leading mark, `space-sm`, title in `AppTheme.data` (13 / 500): `on-surface`, or the row's tone when it states one; then 2pt and a detail in `chrome` 11 / 600 `on-surface-variant`; an optional trailing figure in `chrome` 11 / 600, capped at 132pt and ellipsised. Rows are `space-md` apart. Bazarr's wanted rows lead with the title's poster as well, the one the arr Bazarr follows holds (`arrPublicPosterByIdProvider`), or its initials. Leading marks in use: a 58pt day column (`ServiceOverviewDay`: *Today*, *Tomorrow* or the weekday in `figure-s` 20 in the ink, scaled down to fit, the date under it in `caption` 12 in the secondary ink, *21 Oct*), a title's 36 × 54 poster (`ServiceOverviewArt`: a public poster, glowing with its own light once drawn, or the title's initials, *NS*, in the label role 600 in the secondary ink on `fill-strong` with a `line-edge` edge, `radius-sm`, scaled down into the plate at any text size, never out of it), the two side by side for what is coming, an 18px tone glyph, an 8px tone dot set 5pt down. Tones walk against `surface-container-low`.
- **A row that leads somewhere** (`ServiceOverviewRow(onTap:, doorHint:)`): a row is only read unless it is given a door, and a row with neither a door nor a verb is exactly the row above. A door is the place the row is about: the whole row is the target, at least 44 tall (the `space-md` under the row sits inside it, so the rows keep their rhythm), with the ink response at `radius-sm` and an 18px `chevron_right` in `on-surface-variant` after the figure. It is one button for a screen reader: the sentence as the label, `doorHint` after it (*opens the Prowlarr Indexers tab*, *opens this download*), the parts silenced. `onTap` and `doorHint` come together; a door with no words for where it goes is refused. Where a door goes is data (`AttentionDoor`, `lib/features/services/domain/attention_door.dart`), opened by `openAttentionDoor`: a sibling tab by `goBranch`, as the tab bar does; one download's page through `openDownloadAt`; a page of the service (an indexer, a stack) by the router. Outside a router it does nothing. The door is the most specific place the app has: the item where it has a page, else the tab that lists it, else no door.
- **A verb beside a row** (`ServiceOverviewRowAction(label:, hint:, onPressed:)`): something the app already does elsewhere, one tap from the row (*Test* an indexer, *Start* a stack), through the feature's own runner. A text button in the ink (`primary`, `label-large` 600), never the fault's tone: the cure is not painted as alarming as the disease. It sits outside the row's sentence, centred on the row, `space-sm` off the text; 44 tall, at least 56 wide and at most 45% of the row (a long label such as *Look for peers again* ellipsises and the words keep the rest, as the trailing figure does), with `VisualDensity.standard` pinned, since the desktop picks compact by itself and compact takes 4 off `minimumSize`. Its hint is annotated on the label inside the button (*tests Nyaa*), never by a `Semantics` around it. With a verb the chevron is not drawn: the verb is the mark, as on a Home decision row, and the body stays tappable.
- **An amber fault's remedy** (`ServiceOverviewRowAction.remedy(…)`): a stalled torrent's *Look for peers again*, a failing indexer's *Test*. The one amber fill (`remedyFillStyle`, compact), as the Home's Downloads draw the same remedy, placed under the row's words at the words' inset (the 18pt glyph and `space-sm`), `space-md` under it, so a long machine name keeps the row's width. The plain verb beside a row stays for a row that is not a fault (a stopped stack's *Start*).
- **Folded rows**: several faults of one kind on one service are one row (*3 indexers failing*, the names in the detail, up to three and *and 2 more*), whose door is the list that shows them all; one of a kind keeps its own row and its own door. A count taken inside a read that stopped short is a floor (*3+ downloads stuck*, spoken *at least 3*). The section's own count still counts what the rows stand for, not the rows.
- **Empty** (`ServiceOverviewEmpty`): `AppEmptyState.inline`: a row, not a well: nothing failed. Radarr's: *Nothing releasing this week*, *Nothing needs you*, *Nothing added yet*, *Nothing yet today*.
- **Skeleton** (`ServiceOverviewRowsSkeleton`): `rows` × (a 180 × 14 bar, `space-xs`, a 120 × 10 bar; `radius-xs`, `surface-container-high` shimmering to `surface-container`), `space-md` apart. A poster rail loads as `AppSkeleton.posterRow`. A subtitle count reads `—` while its source is loading or failed.
- **Failure**: each section is an `AsyncValueWidget.section` of its own (`.bleedSection` under `ServiceOverviewSection.bleed`). A source that never answered draws one `InlineErrorRetry` line in that section's slot, under the header that still stands: *Plex answered with an error, so what the server says about itself is not shown.*, never the page-sized `AppErrorState`; one that answered before and failed on refresh keeps its rows under a `FaultBand`, and its count keeps counting them. The readouts follow the same rule: a library that did not answer turns every figure built on it to `—` with the note *Radarr · Did not answer* (or *Radarr · Refused the key*, the classified reason), while the queue and history sections still draw.
- **Only at home** (`sectionsOrOneLine`, `ServiceOverviewOnlyAtHome`): away from home, a lens connection with no address away asks nothing, and every section would fail for the one same reason. The page says it once instead: the identity line reads `home` *Only at home* in neutral, the figure peek and the sections give way to one `InlineErrorRetry.flush` on the page column (`space-sm` top, `space-lg` sides and bottom), *Radarr has no address away, so Cupola reaches it only on your home Wi-Fi.*, with *Add an address away* in ink, which opens the connection's form on that empty field, focused. No Retry: the same request is refused until the app is home. With two connections it names the lens's (*Radarr 4K has no address away…*), or the connection the page reads when it is given one (`instanceId`). A spread, not a wrapper, so the page's list still builds its sections lazily.
- **Neither address answered** (`sectionsOrOneLine`, `ServiceOverviewUnanswered`): a lens connection with an address away that the route probe found silent at both addresses. Every section would say the same red, so the page says it once, in the same slot and spacing as *Only at home*: the identity line reads `cloud_off` *Did not answer* in red, then one `InlineErrorRetry.flush`, *Radarr did not answer at home or away.* and under it the two addresses, one a line, *At home · nas.home.arpa:7878* and *Away · radarr.example.net*, with *Retry* a ghost in ink that knocks at both addresses again. With two connections it names the lens's, or the connection the page reads when it is given one.
- **Did not answer** (`sectionsOrOneLine`, `ServiceOverviewUnanswered.atItsAddress`): the probe of a lens connection did not answer at the address it asked (the one it has, or the one its route is on). The same one line in the same slot: *Radarr did not answer at nas.home.arpa:7878.* in red, with *Retry* a ghost in ink. A section whose own source failed while the probe answered still says it in its slot (above). Stream's Overview keeps its own one state for a dark server (`ServiceOfflineState`, drawn when its own reads failed), so it passes `ownSilence: true`.
- **A media server's visit** (Jellyfin, Plex, Emby): the Overview reads one connection, the one its visit has in view (`ConnectionInViewScope`: what a row naming a connection opened the service on, or what the lens sheet chose, else the lens), and every region on the page reads it: the identity line, the figures, Now playing, the glance at what is being watched (*Continue watching*, *On deck* on Plex, and *Recently added*: a rail each, wide pictures with how far in and posters, leading to Activity where the shelf is whole; `StreamWatchingRail`, decision `P-stream-library-browse`), the shelf and the server facts. A watch state with no household member chosen on Jellyfin or Emby reads *Choose whose watch state to show* with *Pick a viewer*, never an empty rail. `sectionsOrOneLine` takes the same connection (`instanceId`), so *did not answer* and *only at home* are about the connection on screen and not about the lens behind it. A page whose header names one connection and whose figures come from another is the one thing it must not be.
- **Tracearr** (`TracearrOverviewScreen`, in the Stream band and not a media server): the readouts are its own (*Streams*, *Today*, *Unseen*, *Servers*; *Unseen* is a floor, `25+`, when another page follows, and a figure whose read did not answer is `—`), then four sections that load and fail on their own: *Monitored servers* (each server by the lexicon's words, `Answering` green, `Did not answer Tracearr` red, `Refused Tracearr's token` amber with the remedy `Fix it in Tracearr`, and Tracearr's own `unknown` a dash, never a state), *Playing now* (the first three streams of Live, the very list the *Streams* readout counts, kept moving while the page is on screen; each a door to the stream's detail sheet, which opens the title of a movie by its TMDB id and of an episode by its series' ids, found in one read of the show Tracearr names), *Violations* (the newest, severity in words and blue, each row a door to the violation's page) and *Most watched this week*, which is left out altogether on a Tracearr that has no such read (older than 3.1.4), never drawn empty. `ServiceOverviewArt` takes the headers a picture's own server wants (Tracearr's key travels there and never in the address).
- **Tone**: stalled and import failed are faults (`warning`); films below cutoff are backlog (neutral). In History, grabbed is neutral and imported is `success`.

Nothing takes a colour: callers pass the `ServiceKey`, so a hue can never become text. `serviceOverviewInk(service, colorScheme)` (the secondary ink) is the one public way to ink a page's own marks the same way. Each row speaks one sentence with `·` turned into commas and silences its parts (a door speaks it as a button, with where it goes as the hint; a verb is a button of its own beside it); the identity speaks *Radarr, host, version, Answering[, lens]*; a linked header speaks its title and subtitle with the hint *opens the Radarr Calendar tab*. A screen checks `isServiceConfigured` before any section is built and otherwise shows `NotConfiguredPlaceholder.forService`.

- **A Tracearr violation's page** (`TracearrViolationScreen`, `/stack/tracearr/violations/:id`, resolved from the path alone, `?i=` naming the connection; an id that is not a UUID is *Violation not found* with no request): the page's name is the rule, under it the severity in words and when. Three slabs built from `ServiceOverviewSection` and `ServiceOverviewRow`: *Violation* (the rule, blue while unseen and neutral once acknowledged, the severity at its end; the person and the server; *Triggered by a stream* or *Not tied to one stream*), *What Tracearr did* (a row an automatic action, its result at the end: `Done`, `Skipped` with Tracearr's reason, or `Failed`, which is the one fault on the page, the glyph and the title amber with what failed under it; no action reads *Tracearr took no automatic action*) and *What it saw*. What it saw is typed for the two rules Cupola knows, told by the condition Tracearr 3.1.4 weighed and not by the rule's name (`travel_speed_kmh`: the two places as city and country, then the distance and the time between; `concurrent_streams`: how many at once and the limit) and for any other rule it is the one line *Only Tracearr shows what this rule saw*: the free data is never drawn as keys and values. Under the slabs, `Acknowledge` (a filled button, gone once acknowledged) and `Dismiss` (outlined), the owner's, through `GatedAffordance.builder`: with no owner signed in both are dimmed to 38%, keep their 44pt target and a real tap, carry the hint *Sign in to Tracearr as the owner* and lead to the connection's settings; with an owner they act at full ink, `Dismiss` after a neutral confirmation (*Dismiss this violation?*, `Dismiss violation` / `Keep it`); the owner is the session `TracearrOwnerFields` signs in, through the connection's route. A Tracearr that signs in only with single sign-on, with nobody signed in, draws no dimmed control at all: one blue `NoticeLine` stands where they would be, *This Tracearr signs in with single sign-on, which Cupola can't use.* · *Acknowledge it in Tracearr.* A violation Tracearr no longer has is said (*Tracearr no longer has this violation*), never a failure. An answer that says this Tracearr has no such route, or one in a shape Cupola does not read, takes both off the page, and off every violation page for the rest of the run. Last, `Open in Tracearr` (outlined, opens the violation in the browser at the address the connection is reached on).

- Tracearr's whole screens, on the demo world's fake Tracearr (`test/design_catalog/tracearr_world.dart`, over the world `emby_world.dart` starts), with the live feed off and three reads written by the scene (a refused server, a transcode, an unseen floor): `TracearrOverviewScreen` (the readouts with *Unseen* a floor and *Servers 2 of 3*, one server refused Tracearr's token in amber with *Fix it in Tracearr*, Playing now, *Latest violations* (headed so, since its link to the Violations tab reads the tab's name), Most watched this week); `TracearrLiveScreen` (the summary line over a transcode with the server's reason, a paused stream and a direct one); the session sheet over Live (how it reaches the viewer, source against output, the facts, *Open title*, *Go to Anna*, *Stop stream*), drawn in place as `AppBottomSheet` around `TracearrSessionBody`, since a modal on the root navigator is off the sheet; `TracearrHistoryScreen` (day-grouped, with a remembered filter on the filter row, whole on as many lines as it needs, never a row to scroll, so every active filter and *Clear* show on a phone); `TracearrUsersScreen`; `TracearrPersonScreen` (7 days, 30 days and all time, genres, latest viewings, the Violations link); `TracearrViolationsScreen` (Unseen and the severity pills).

- `TracearrViolationScreen`: an unseen impossible-travel violation with a failed action and the owner controls dimmed; a rule Cupola does not read, acknowledged, with the owner signed in; single sign-on the only way in, with the blue line where the controls would be.
- Text at 200%: loaded, the library timed out, the row shapes, and Tracearr's Overview.

## Do / don't

- Do pass the `ServiceKey` and let the furniture resolve every ink; never hand it a colour.
- Do let a section fail in its own slot; a retry re-asks every source that section depends on.
- Don't count inside a capped or paged response and state it as a total; a figure that did not arrive reads `—`, never 0.
- Don't paint the backlog amber: cutoff unmet and wanted are neutral.
