# MediaPage

The one page a title has, whatever service it was opened from: who the title is, the one thing to do with it, and what every server of the household knows about it, each in its own row.

Every movie and series opens on it, under the tab it is opened from: Radarr's and Sonarr's libraries and Overviews, Seerr's lists and requests, the search, the Dashboard and the queue, and a media server's movie or series that carries its ids; a season or an episode a media server lists opens its series' page, on that season. A link that names a title by an arr's own id goes through `ArrTitleOpener` (`lib/features/media/presentation/arr_title_opener.dart`), which asks that arr which title it is and takes its place; a series opens by TMDB's id wherever a source can name it (a Sonarr v3 record through Seerr's `tvdb:` search), by TVDB's only when none can.

Rendered from `lib/features/media/presentation/media_page.dart` (`MediaPage`) `media_add_sheet.dart` (`MediaAddSheet`) `media_copy_panel_card.dart` (`MediaCopyPanelCard`), `media_decision_card.dart` (`MediaDecisionCard`), `media_facts_sections.dart` (`MediaWatchProviders`, `MediaReleaseDates`) and `media_series_sections.dart` (`MediaNextAiringCard`, `MediaEpisodes`), `media_series_manage.dart` (`MediaSeriesManage`), `media_episode_sheet.dart` (`MediaEpisodeSheet`), `media_episodes_graph.dart` (`MediaEpisodesGraphPanel`, `MediaEpisodesGraphSheet`), with a series' seasons, next airing and next episode from `lib/features/media/domain/media_series.dart`, with the rows' words from `lib/features/media/domain/media_row_view.dart`, the lead from `lib/features/media/domain/media_lead.dart`, the identity, scores and marks from `lib/features/media/presentation/media_identity.dart`, the closed sections from `media_manage_summaries.dart` and `lib/features/media/domain/media_series_manage.dart`, Bazarr's words from `media_subtitles.dart` and the light from `lib/core/title_light.dart`, by `test/design_catalog/media_page_catalog_test.dart`.

A movie a media server lists (its libraries, On deck, search) opens here when the server knows its TMDB id (`MediaKey.ofStreamItem`, `openStreamItem`), with that server's copy in focus; no poster flies from those tiles, since the server's artwork needs its credential. A season or an episode opens its series' page on that season: the tap reads the series through the connection's own client (`StreamServerClient.getSeriesKey`: its TMDB id, else TVDB's) and takes the page when the server names it within four seconds. An album, a track, a movie with no TMDB id, an item that names no series, a series the server holds no ids for, and a read that failed keep the server's own page, with no word about the read. Everything else is read: `mediaRowsProvider`, the audience, the settings. Titles, figures and times in the catalog are illustrative; the light in the catalog is computed by `titleLightFromPixels` from the catalog's own artwork before the sheet is drawn.

Semantics: the title is the page's header; each row speaks one sentence (`Plex. Did not answer since 18:12. Last known: 1080p · Available. Retry`) and is one button when it leads somewhere, as is the collection (`Silent Horror, 5 of 8 on your server`); the scores speak `Rotten Tomatoes: 97`, `Metacritic: no score`. The artwork and the plate are silent.

Do: let the row decide its own shape and words (`mediaRowView`). Don't: paint a service's accent, draw a not-set-up service as answering, or build a control whose promise the page cannot keep: without Seerr, Trailer and Watchlist step aside.

## States in the catalog

Each sheet is drawn in the dark and the light room.

- Operator, the full household: Resume on Jellyfin lit by the backdrop, a fact with its picture, a failure with its last answer, two Radarr copies, Bazarr, Seerr not set up.
- Requester: the media servers only, no Manage, no Metacritic; and Request, over a title not on the server yet.
- No backdrop (the poster, veiled), no artwork (the plate), waiting.
- Media server only: no Seerr, no arr; the server names the title, with its synopsis and its poster.
- Series: Resume the episode, the next to air, Sonarr's facts one row each (a stuck import with its remedy), the episodes a season at a time.
- Series on a season: opened from a season or an episode of a media server, the episodes open on that season, not the one Play continues.
